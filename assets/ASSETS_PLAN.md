# Asset plan
Use original or properly licensed assets only.
- Character: original low-poly cyber-runner GLB, target 8k–20k triangles, shared material atlas where possible.
- Environment: modular city blocks and reusable road segments.
- Obstacles: 3–5 readable silhouettes with emissive accents.
- Collectible: energy ring/coin mesh.
- Audio: original/licensed UI click, coin pickup, jump, collision and short music loop.
- UI: CSS/SVG and original icons; do not use third-party game logos or characters without permission.
- Textures: mostly 512–1024 px and compressed for production.
